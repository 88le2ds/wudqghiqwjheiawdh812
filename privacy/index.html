Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 June 2024

This policy explains how Silverheart Studios handles information you voluntarily provide through this website.

Information you provide

If you create a profile or submit music, you may provide contact details, links, location, genre preferences, and messages. We use this information only for your submission or account request.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites, basic technical information may be collected by the hosting service, such as browser type, device information, pages viewed, and approximate location derived from an IP address. This helps keep the site secure and understand how it is used.

How we use information

We may use your information to review submissions, respond to enquiries, communicate about your account, prevent misuse, and improve the website. We do not sell personal information or use submitted music for advertising.

Storage and sharing

This demonstration site stores profile details in your browser session only. If a live submission service is connected in future, we will update this policy to describe its storage and retention practices. We may share information only where required by law, to protect our rights and users, or with service providers that help us operate the website.

Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, unless a longer period is required by law. You can request deletion of information you submitted by contacting us.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information you have provided by emailing us. You can also opt out of non-essential updates at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under local law.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through this website.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@silverheart.cc.
